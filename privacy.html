Privacy Policy

Elyssa Content OS · Effective date: September 2026 · Contact: business@elyssia-agency.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Elyssa Content OS ("Elyssa", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards information when you use our platform, including our website, dashboard, and any connected integrations (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information in order to operate the Service:

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, agency or organization name, and any profile details you choose to provide.
  • Connected social accounts. When you voluntarily connect a third-party platform (such as TikTok or Instagram), we receive account identifiers, basic profile information, and the access tokens and permissions necessary to perform the actions you authorize (for example, publishing or scheduling content).
  • Uploaded media. We process the video files, images, captions, and associated metadata that you or your connected storage provider (such as Google Drive) upload to the Service for editing, transcription, and publishing.
  • Usage analytics. We collect information about how you interact with the Service, including feature usage, session activity, device and browser type, and performance metrics, to help us maintain and improve the platform.

How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Content processing. Transcribe, edit, render, and prepare uploaded media for publication.
  • Scheduling. Plan and organize content across campaigns and connected accounts on your behalf.
  • Publishing. Publish or schedule content to the third-party platforms you have explicitly connected and authorized.
  • Platform functionality. Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service, including troubleshooting, analytics, and customer support.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use uploaded media or connected account data for any purpose beyond operating the Service and delivering the features you have requested.

Third-Party Services

Elyssa integrates with the following third-party services to provide core functionality:

  • TikTok — for publishing and managing content on connected TikTok accounts.
  • Instagram (Meta) — for publishing and managing content on connected Instagram accounts.
  • Google Drive — for ingesting raw video files from a connected storage folder.
  • Supabase — as our underlying database and infrastructure provider, used to securely store account, content, and scheduling data.

Users voluntarily connect these services. Each connection is established through the provider's own authorization flow (such as OAuth), and you may disconnect any third-party service at any time from your account settings. Your use of each third-party platform remains subject to that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

Data Retention

We retain account information and content data for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide the Service. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information and uploaded media within a commercially reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Access tokens for connected social accounts are deleted immediately upon disconnection or account deletion.

Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encryption of data in transit, access controls restricting who within our organization can access production data, and reliance on infrastructure providers that maintain industry-standard security certifications. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information and account data;
  • Withdraw consent and disconnect any third-party service at any time;
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable timeframe and in accordance with applicable law.
